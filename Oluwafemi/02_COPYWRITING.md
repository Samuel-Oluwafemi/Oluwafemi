# ABOUT

Eyebrow:

A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Headline:

# I care about what happens after the page loads.

Copy:

I started by learning the web from the ground up — HTML, CSS and JavaScript — before moving into React and modern frontend development.

Over time, I became increasingly interested in what happens beyond the code.

Why does one website immediately make a business feel trustworthy while another creates friction?

Why does one landing page make the next step obvious while another leaves visitors wondering what to do?

Today, I combine frontend development with UI/UX thinking and conversion-focused website strategy.

I like taking an idea, making the experience clear, building it and getting it into people's hands.
