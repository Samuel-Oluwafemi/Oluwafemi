# Oluwafemi — Brand Guidelines

## Brand Name

Primary name:

**Samuel Oluwafemi**

Developer identity:

**Oluwafemi**

The X can occasionally become part of the visual identity.

---

# Brand Idea

## Build like a developer. Think like a product designer.

Oluwafemi represents the combination of:

**Code + Design + UX + Business Thinking**

The portfolio should communicate that Samuel is interested in the complete experience, not just implementation.

---

# Brand Personality

### 01 — Curious

Always exploring better ways to build.

### 02 — Practical

Technology should solve real problems.

### 03 — Ambitious

The work should communicate forward movement.

### 04 — Creative

The developer is allowed to have taste.

### 05 — Human

Avoid sounding like an AI-generated corporate résumé.

### 06 — Confident

Show the work without overclaiming.

---

# Visual Identity

The visual identity should feel like:

**A developer's private digital studio opened to the public.**

Imagine entering a dark creative workspace where:

- projects are pinned around the room
- build logs are visible
- interfaces are being tested
- typography dominates the walls
- technical notes sit beside visual work

Translate that concept into a refined digital interface.

---

# Logo Direction

Do not create a complicated logo.

Use:

**Oluwafemi✦**

with the ✦ subtly emphasized.

Possible treatment:

`Oluwafemi` + highlighted `✦`

The logo should work in:

- navbar
- favicon
- project metadata
- footer
- social preview

---

# Visual Motifs

Use these recurring motifs:

### Build labels

`BUILD 001`

`BUILD 002`

`BUILD 003`

### Project labels

`CLIENT PROJECT`

`PERSONAL PROJECT`

`CONCEPT`

`IN PROGRESS`

`SHIPPED`

### Technical metadata

`REACT`

`FIREBASE`

`TAILWIND`

`UX`

`CRO`

These should be small supporting details.

Never let them dominate the interface.

---

# Voice

Write like a confident young developer who actually builds.

Use:

- short sentences
- clear language
- direct statements
- occasional personality

Avoid:

"I am a highly motivated and passionate developer..."

Instead:

"I build digital experiences..."

Avoid:

"I have extensive experience..."

when it is not supported.

The portfolio should be honest about Samuel's current stage while still communicating serious ambition.

---

# Brand Statement

Primary:

> I build digital experiences that make businesses easier to trust, use and buy from.

Alternative:

> I turn ideas and business problems into clear, modern digital experiences.

---

# Emotional Goal

The visitor should feel:

**"This person has taste."**

Then:

**"This person can actually build."**

Then:

**"I could work with him."**
